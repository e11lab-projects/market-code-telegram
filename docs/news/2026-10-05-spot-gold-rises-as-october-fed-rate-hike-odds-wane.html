Spot gold climbed 0.6% to $4,165.49 after a soft September jobs report lowered tightening expectations. Reduced chances of a Fed rate hike lessen yield competition against non-yielding bullion.

តម្លៃមាស Spot បានកើនឡើង 0.6% ដល់ 4,165.49 ដុល្លារ បន្ទាប់ពីរបាយការណ៍ការងារខែកញ្ញាខ្សោយបានកាត់បន្ថយការរំពឹងទុកលើការរឹតបន្តឹង។ ការធ្លាក់ចុះនៃឱកាសដំឡើងអត្រាការប្រាក់របស់ Fed ជួយកាត់បន្ថយសម្ពាធលើលោហៈធាតុដែលមិនផ្តល់ផលប័ត្រ។

Spot gold advanced 0.6% to reach $4,165.49 per ounce on Monday following the release of a weaker-than-anticipated September employment report. The softer labor data prompted market participants to scale back expectations for further monetary policy tightening by the Federal Reserve, with traders pricing in only an 18% probability of a rate hike in October.

The primary channel connecting labor market data to gold operates through monetary policy expectations and opportunity costs. When economic reports reveal cooling job market conditions, expectations for central bank interest rate increases tend to diminish. As market odds for near-term rate hikes fall, the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding precious metals declines, generally enhancing gold's appeal relative to interest-bearing assets.

In this market environment, gold typically finds sustained upward support if subsequent economic releases continue to signal moderation, reinforcing expectations that the Federal Reserve will pause or conclude its policy tightening cycle. Conversely, if upcoming data indicators demonstrate renewed economic resilience or persistent inflation, expectations for additional rate hikes could rebound, which would typically exert downward pressure on gold prices.
