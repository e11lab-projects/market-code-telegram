A joint report reveals digital gold could unlock up to $700 billion in latent demand globally. Expanding investor access through tokenized channels could bolster long-term structural demand for gold.

របាយការណ៍រួមគ្នាមួយបង្ហាញថាមាសបែបឌីជីថលអាចទាក់ទាញតម្រូវការវិនិយោគរហូតដល់ 700 ពាន់លានដុល្លារអាមេរិក។ ការពង្រីកលទ្ធភាពវិនិយោគនេះអាចជួយគាំទ្រដល់តម្រូវការមាសរូបវន្តរយៈពេលវែងយ៉ាងខ្លាំង។

A research report released by the World Gold Council alongside Boston Consulting Group highlights an expanding structural opportunity for the precious metals industry. According to the findings, the digital gold market possesses the potential to unlock up to $700 billion in latent investment demand globally, marking a potential shift in how private and institutional wealth accesses bullion.

The transmission channel to gold operates primarily through structural market access and sustained physical backing. Digitizing gold holdings removes traditional hurdles related to storage logistics, minimum investment sizes, and transaction fees. When investors purchase verified digital representations of bullion, issuers typically accumulate allocated physical gold reserves to maintain one-to-one backing, effectively channeling new capital into the spot XAU/USD ecosystem alongside traditional central bank reserves and exchange-traded funds.

If digital gold platforms achieve broad regulatory clearance and user adoption, the steady conversion of latent interest into vaulted bullion could provide consistent structural support for XAU/USD, mitigating the headwinds created by a firm US dollar or elevated US bond yields. On the other hand, if regulatory uncertainty, cybersecurity challenges, or competition from other digital assets delay widespread integration, the projected $700 billion demand may remain largely unrealized, keeping gold valuations tied strictly to macroeconomic data and interest rate expectations.

Traders should monitor how major financial institutions implement digital gold frameworks and whether institutional custody standards align with global regulations. In the interim, market participants will continue watching US economic releases, the direction of the US dollar, and Federal Reserve policy commentary to assess short-term bullion sentiment.
