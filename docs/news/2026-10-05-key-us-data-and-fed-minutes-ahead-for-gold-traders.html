Gold traders face key macro drivers this week, beginning Monday at 21:00 Phnom Penh time with the US ISM Services PMI. Market consensus forecasts a reading of 55.1, following the previous 55.4. Because the services sector constitutes a dominant share of the economy, a figure stronger than forecast typically reinforces the US dollar and elevates real yields, which can weigh on non-yielding bullion. Conversely, a softer reading often dampens interest rate expectations, softening the dollar and offering support to gold prices.
