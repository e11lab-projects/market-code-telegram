China's economic growth has shown signs of acceleration, driven primarily by an artificial intelligence boom that has lifted export performance. This surge in external shipments has provided a crucial counterbalance to subdued domestic consumer spending within the country. Nonetheless, broader economic momentum faces ongoing headwinds, as simmering trade tensions and emerging uncertainties around the sustainability of the AI expansion continue to cloud the outlook.

Persistent trade friction also affects global risk sentiment, which can either strengthen the US dollar as an alternative haven or bolster bullion as a store of value.
