Fed’s Williams Sees 2026 Hike but No 2027 Increasesលោក Williams របស់ Fed រំពឹងដំឡើងការប្រាក់នៅឆ្នាំ 2026 តែគ្មាននៅឆ្នាំ 2027

XAU/USD impact: ◆ Mixedផលប៉ះពាល់លើ XAU/USD: ◆ ចម្រុះ

New York Fed President John Williams expects one more rate hike in 2026 and none in 2027. Shifting interest rate expectations directly influence dollar strength and holding costs for gold.

ប្រធាន Fed សាខា New York លោក John Williams មើលឃើញការដំឡើងអត្រាការប្រាក់មួយលើកទៀតក្នុងឆ្នាំ 2026 ប៉ុន្តែគ្មានការដំឡើងបន្ថែមនៅឆ្នាំ 2027 ឡើយ។ ទស្សនវិស័យគោលនយោបាយនេះមានឥទ្ធិពលផ្ទាល់លើថ្លៃដើមនៃការកាន់កាប់ និងទិសដៅនៃតម្លៃមាស។

New York Fed President John Williams outlined a monetary policy outlook that contrasts with prevailing financial market assumptions. According to Williams, the Federal Reserve may deliver one more interest rate hike in 2026, but will likely avoid any additional rate increases in 2027 as broader inflation pressures ease. This perspective places the policymaker at odds with traders who hold differing expectations for the central bank's tightening trajectory.

ប្រធាន Fed សាខា New York លោក John Williams បានបង្ហាញទស្សនវិស័យគោលនយោបាយរូបិយវត្ថុ ដែលខុសគ្នាពីការរំពឹងទុករបស់ទីផ្សារហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ។ យោងតាមលោក Williams ធនាគារកណ្តាល Fed អាចនឹងដំឡើងអត្រាការប្រាក់មួយលើកទៀតក្នុងឆ្នាំ 2026 ប៉ុន្តែទំនងជានឹងមិនមានការដំឡើងបន្ថែមទៀតនោះទេនៅក្នុងឆ្នាំ 2027 ខណៈដែលសម្ពាធអតិផរណាបានថមថយចុះ។ ទស្សនៈនេះបានធ្វើឱ្យលោកមានជំហរផ្ទុយពីអ្នកជួញដូរ ដែលមានការរំពឹងទុកផ្សេងអំពីវដ្តនៃការរឹតបន្តឹងគោលនយោបាយ។

For gold (XAU/USD), monetary policy projections serve as a primary driver of price momentum through interest rate expectations, the US dollar, and non-yielding asset holding costs. When central bank officials signal further rate increases, short-term yields tend to rise, which typically strengthens the US dollar and raises the opportunity cost of maintaining positions in physical bullion. Conversely, a clearly defined ceiling on future rate adjustments can alleviate structural headwinds for precious metals over a longer horizon.

The dual nature of Williams' guidance creates a balanced set of scenarios for market participants. If inflation pressures continue to ease in line with his baseline view, verifying that monetary policy will not need further tightening in 2027, gold could find underlying support as bond yields stabilize and the US dollar loses upward momentum. However, if persistent price pressures derail the forecast of easing inflation, prompting policymakers to consider further rate hikes beyond the projected 2026 move, bullion could face renewed selling pressure as rate expectations adjust higher.

Looking ahead, market participants will focus closely on incoming inflation reports to evaluate whether price pressures are indeed cooling as Williams anticipates. Traders will also assess forthcoming remarks from other central bank officials to see whether broader committee sentiment aligns with his projection of a single 2026 hike or reflects the alternative path priced in by financial markets.
