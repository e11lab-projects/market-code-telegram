Gold Drops for Third Day on Oil-Driven Inflation Fearsមាសធ្លាក់ចុះជាថ្ងៃទីបី ដោយសារក្តីបារម្ភអតិផរណាពីតម្លៃប្រេង

XAU/USD impact: ▼ Bearishផលប៉ះពាល់លើ XAU/USD: ▼ ធ្លាក់

Gold prices fell on Tuesday, extending declines into a third session as elevated energy costs reignited inflation worries. The shifting Federal Reserve interest rate outlook directly pressures the non-yielding metal ahead of next week's policy meeting.

តម្លៃមាសបានធ្លាក់ចុះនៅថ្ងៃអង្គារ ដោយបន្តការខាតបង់ជាថ្ងៃទីបីជាប់ៗគ្នា ខណៈតម្លៃប្រេងឡើងថ្លៃបានបង្កើនការព្រួយបារម្ភអំពីអតិផរណា។ ទស្សនវិស័យគោលនយោបាយរបស់ Fed ដែលកំពុងផ្លាស់ប្តូរនេះ ប៉ះពាល់ដោយផ្ទាល់ដល់លោហៈធាតុដែលគ្មានការប្រាក់នេះ មុនការប្រជុំធនាគារកណ្តាលសប្តាហ៍ក្រោយ។

Gold prices moved lower on Tuesday, marking a third consecutive day of declines across the bullion market. The persistent selling pressure came as elevated crude oil prices raised fresh concerns over lingering inflation, complicating the path forward for United States monetary policy just days before the Federal Reserve gathers for its scheduled meeting next week.

តម្លៃមាសបានធ្លាក់ចុះបន្តិចនៅថ្ងៃអង្គារ ដែលជាការធ្លាក់ចុះសម្រាប់វគ្គជួញដូរទីបីជាប់ៗគ្នានៅលើទីផ្សារលោហៈធាតុដ៏មានតម្លៃ។ សម្ពាធលក់ជាបន្តបន្ទាប់នេះបានកើតឡើងនៅពេលដែលតម្លៃប្រេងឆៅស្ថិតក្នុងកម្រិតខ្ពស់បានបង្កើនការព្រួយបារម្ភអំពីអតិផរណា និងធ្វើឱ្យទស្សនវិស័យគោលនយោបាយរូបិយវត្ថុរបស់សហរដ្ឋអាមេរិកកាន់តែស្រពិចស្រពិល នៅមុនពេលកិច្ចប្រជុំរបស់ Federal Reserve ឬ Fed នៅសប្តាហ៍ក្រោយ។

The transmission channel connecting energy markets and gold runs directly through interest rate expectations and real yields. When oil prices surge, the risk of broad-based price pressures increases, forcing financial markets to reassess the likelihood and timing of monetary easing by the Fed. Because gold is a non-yielding asset, the prospect of prolonged restrictive monetary policy tends to support US Treasury yields and the US dollar, diminishing the immediate investment appeal of holding bullion.

Looking forward, market direction will heavily depend on whether energy costs continue to climb and how central bankers interpret those inflationary pressures. If elevated fuel prices translate into stickier consumer prices and prompt Fed policymakers to maintain a more hawkish policy stance, gold could face continued downward momentum. Conversely, if high energy prices begin to threaten broader economic growth or if the Federal Reserve signals comfort looking past supply-side price spikes, safe-haven demand and hedge buying could re-emerge to support gold prices.

In the coming days, traders will be closely monitoring crude oil price trends and any fresh inflation indicators leading directly into the Federal Reserve's upcoming rate decision. Market participants will also scrutinize communications from central bank officials and any adjustments in interest rate pricing across financial markets to gauge the potential trajectory for XAU/USD.
