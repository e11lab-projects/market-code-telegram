UK annual inflation climbed to a higher-than-expected 3.5% through September, propelled by rising motor fuel prices. The resulting shift in interest rate expectations influences both currency valuations and gold demand.

អតិផរណាប្រចាំឆ្នាំនៅ UK បានកើនឡើងដល់ 3.5% ក្នុងខែកញ្ញា ដោយសារថ្លៃប្រេងឥន្ធនៈហក់ឡើងខ្ពស់។ បម្រែបម្រួលនៃការរំពឹងទុកលើអត្រាការប្រាក់នេះ ជះឥទ្ធិពលលើតម្លៃរូបិយប័ណ្ណ និងតម្រូវការមាស។

Inflation in the United Kingdom accelerated to 3.5% across the 12 months leading into September, exceeding consensus expectations. The official uptick in consumer prices was largely driven by the surging cost of motor fuels, reinforcing indications of renewed price pressures within the domestic economy.

អតិផរណានៅចក្រភពអង់គ្លេសបានកើនឡើងដល់ 3.5% ក្នុងរយៈពេល 12 ខែគិតត្រឹមខែកញ្ញា ដែលជាកម្រិតខ្ពស់ជាងការរំពឹងទុក។ ការកើនឡើងនៃសន្ទស្សន៍ថ្លៃទំនិញប្រើប្រាស់នេះ ត្រូវបានជំរុញជាចម្បងដោយការកើនឡើងយ៉ាងគំហុកនៃថ្លៃប្រេងឥន្ធនៈយានយន្ត ដែលបញ្ជាក់ពីសម្ពាធថ្លៃទំនិញនៅក្នុងសេដ្ឋកិច្ច។

The hotter-than-expected figure has cemented expectations for additional interest rate hikes from monetary policymakers. In currency markets, rising rate expectations typically support the domestic currency, which can exert downward pressure on the US dollar if foreign exchange traders adjust their cross-currency positions accordingly.

For gold (XAU/USD), macroeconomic dynamics from major international economies flow primarily through currency valuations and global yield trends. A firmer British pound that softens the US dollar index can provide indirect support to bullion by making dollar-denominated assets less expensive for international market participants.

សម្រាប់ទីផ្សារមាស (XAU/USD) ឥទ្ធិពលម៉ាក្រូសេដ្ឋកិច្ចពីប្រទេសធំៗឆ្លងកាត់តាមរយៈតម្លៃរូបិយប័ណ្ណ និងទិន្នផលសញ្ញាបណ្ណសកល។ ការឡើងថ្លៃនៃរូបិយប័ណ្ណផោនដែលធ្វើឱ្យដុល្លារអាមេរិកចុះខ្សោយ អាចផ្តល់ការគាំទ្រដោយប្រយោលដល់មាស ដោយសារមាសគិតជា USD មានតម្លៃធូរថ្លៃជាងមុនសម្រាប់អ្នកទិញអន្តរជាតិ។

Conversely, higher policy rate expectations across advanced economies keep upward pressure on sovereign bond yields. Because gold offers no periodic yield, elevated benchmark interest rates tend to weigh on bullion demand as institutional investors evaluate the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets.

If persistent energy and fuel expenses continue to generate broader inflationary pressures, central bank tightening expectations could strengthen further. Traders will continue monitoring upcoming monetary policy communications to assess how rate adjustments may steer currency crosses and precious metals.
