Spot gold climbed to $4,173.59 an ounce and futures rose to $4,194.05 following remarks by Warsh. The comments curbed expectations for aggressive Federal Reserve rate hikes, supporting precious metals.

Spot gold rose 0.2% to reach $4,173.59 an ounce, while U.S. gold futures gained 0.3% to hit $4,194.05. Meanwhile, other precious metals also saw positive movement, with silver advancing 0.6% to $57.3070 an ounce, platinum climbing 0.4% to $1,641.66, and palladium rising 0.8% to $1,125.05. Concurrently, the U.S. Dollar Index remained little changed at 101.82.
