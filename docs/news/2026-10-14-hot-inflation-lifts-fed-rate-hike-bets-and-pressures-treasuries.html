XAU/USD impact: ◆ Mixedផលប៉ះពាល់លើ XAU/USD: ◆ ចម្រុះ

US Treasuries slumped after hotter-than-expected inflation data prompted traders to add to wagers on Federal Reserve interest rate hikes. This shift affects gold as higher yields and rate expectations influence borrowing costs and precious metals demand.

US Treasuries slumped following the release of hotter-than-expected inflation data for September. The consumer-price index report showed that price increases outpaced expectations, driving a sharp reaction across the bond market and shifting expectations regarding monetary policy.

Following the inflation figures, traders quickly added to wagers that Federal Reserve officials will raise interest rates later this month and next year. Market participants heard reactions from industry figures such as David Rosenberg, Founder of Rosenberg Research, and Jim Bianco, Founder of Bianco Research, as the data fueled ongoing debates about the trajectory of consumer prices.

This environment creates a complex transmission channel for gold (XAU/USD). Hotter inflation typically serves as a traditional price hedge, which can support precious metals. However, the immediate market reaction involving slumping Treasuries and rising rate-hike bets often strengthens the US dollar and pushes real yields higher, creating potential headwinds for non-yielding assets like gold.

A balanced view highlights competing forces for traders. On one hand, persistent inflation pressures and concerns that consumers may come to expect stubbornly high prices can renew safe-haven and inflation-hedging interest in gold. On the other hand, aggressive rate-hike expectations and higher bond yields tend to pressure gold prices downward by increasing the opportunity cost of holding bullion.

Traders should watch upcoming economic data releases, central bank commentary, and further movements in the bond market for additional clues on future policy decisions. Monitoring shifts in Treasury yields and the US dollar will remain essential for assessing short-term direction.
