President Trump has waived several sanctions against Russia under a newly enacted law targeting Moscow over the war in Ukraine. Shifts in US sanctions policy directly affect geopolitical risk sentiment and safe-haven demand across the gold market.

ប្រធានាធិបតី Trump បានសម្រេចលើកលែងទណ្ឌកម្មមួយចំនួនប្រឆាំងនឹងរុស្ស៊ីក្រោមច្បាប់ថ្មីរបស់សហរដ្ឋអាមេរិកដែលដាក់ទោសទីក្រុងម៉ូស្គូជុំវិញសង្គ្រាមនៅអ៊ុយក្រែន។ ការប្រែប្រួលនៃគោលនយោបាយទណ្ឌកម្មនេះជះឥទ្ធិពលផ្ទាល់ដល់ហានិភ័យភូមិសាស្ត្រនយោបាយ និងតម្រូវការទិញមាសទុកជាទ្រព្យសុវត្ថិភាព។

As a new United States law intended to penalize Moscow for its ongoing war against Ukraine took effect, President Trump moved to waive several of its sanctions provisions. In justifying the move, Trump declared that specific sections of the punitive legislation run contrary to US national interests.

Geopolitical developments and sanctions policy serve as major transmission mechanisms for financial markets and precious metals like gold (XAU/USD). When international sanctions regimes shift, they alter market perceptions of geopolitical risk and global trade friction. Gold often functions as a defensive hedge against international conflict and political fragmentation, meaning abrupt modifications to punitive measures can recalibrate the geopolitical risk premium embedded in bullion prices.

If the decision to grant waivers is viewed by market participants as a de-escalation of economic conflict or a step toward reducing geopolitical friction surrounding Ukraine, risk appetite could improve across global markets. Under such conditions, safe-haven demand for bullion typically cools, potentially removing an important layer of support for gold prices and allowing macroeconomic factors like bond yields and the US dollar to exert greater downward pressure.

Conversely, if the waiver creates institutional friction between the administration and legislators, or introduces fresh unpredictability regarding United States foreign policy and enforcement, defensive positioning could remain elevated. Persistent uncertainty surrounding international stability and the broader conflict in Ukraine would likely sustain safe-haven interest, offering underlying support to XAU/USD.
